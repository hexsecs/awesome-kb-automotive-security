Shiho Kim and Rakesh Shrestha, Springer 2020. Academic introduction to connected and autonomous vehicle security, threats, and the standardisation landscape.

An academic introduction (full title: *Automotive Cyber Security: Introduction, Challenges, and Standardization*) by two Yonsei University researchers. Its nine chapters move from intelligent autonomous vehicles and their security/privacy problems through in-vehicle communication security, AUTOSAR embedded security, inter-vehicle (V2X) communication security, and the Internet of Vehicles, closing with the standardization landscape.

Best for graduate students and researchers who want a survey-level map of the field's topics before going deeper elsewhere. Caveat: it is a breadth-first academic survey rather than a practitioner's guide, and it appeared in 2020 — before ISO/SAE 21434 was published (2021) — so its standards coverage describes the landscape that standard emerged from, not the standard itself.
