Dennis Kengo Oka, Wiley 2021. Focuses on assuring the automotive software development lifecycle, from secure coding and static analysis through fuzzing and penetration testing.

A process book (subtitle: *Assuring the Automotive Software Development Lifecycle*) by a Chalmers-trained automotive security expert whose career runs through Volvo Cars and Bosch/ESCRYPT. It maps cybersecurity activities onto each stage of the automotive V-model development process: cybersecurity requirements review, automotive-grade secure hardware, secure coding and static analysis, fuzzing, and penetration testing, with a closing emphasis on automating these activities and integrating application-security testing tools into ALM systems for traceability.

Written for software development process owners, developers and engineers, and cybersecurity teams at OEMs and suppliers who need security built into the lifecycle rather than tested in at the end. Caveat: published in 2021, it is a pre-software-defined-vehicle treatment — the lifecycle framing endures, but the architecture it assumes is the distributed-ECU era's.
