Security resources across IoT, embedded, industrial, and automotive systems with a dedicated automotive section.

V33RU's cross-domain collection: 900+ security resources spanning IoT, embedded, industrial control, robotics, and automotive, organised into per-domain documents rather than one long list. The automotive document covers CAN and ECU security material; the neighbouring documents (firmware, wireless, hardware, ICS) are often just as relevant to vehicle work, which is the point of the collection.

Reach for it when a vehicle problem is really a connected-device problem — the automotive section alone is thinner than the dedicated automotive lists, but the embedded and ICS sections carry depth those lists lack. It is a map of several fields at once, so expect to hop between its documents.
