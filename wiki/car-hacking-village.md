Non-profit running hands-on car hacking workshops and CTFs at DEF CON, Black Hat, HITCON, CODE BLUE, and other conferences.

## What it is

The Car Hacking Village (CHV) is a community non-profit — its site states **501(c)(3) status, EIN 92-2131805** — that runs the hands-on car-hacking areas at security conferences. Its programme is beginner workshops, CTF competitions, live automotive reverse engineering and open hacking benches stocked with real ECUs, infotainment units and EV charging infrastructure. Its flagship appearance is DEF CON in Las Vegas, but the events calendar shows a much wider circuit: CODE BLUE and Tengucon in Tokyo, DEF CON Singapore, Hardwear.io NL in Amsterdam, Defcamp in Bucharest, Ekoparty in Buenos Aires, HITCON, and other BSides and regional events.

## How it works

Each village is built around supervised, practical access to hardware rather than lectures. The 2026 events page describes the recurring ingredients:

- **Beginner hands-on workshops** — introductory car hacking, repeatedly described as "for beginners".
- **CTF competitions** — a competitive CTF at DEF CON 34; beginner-friendly CTFs at Defcamp 2025 and the first DEF CON Singapore.
- **Live automotive reverse engineering** — a standard feature at CODE BLUE, with research presentations on a stage at some events.
- **Open hacking areas with real hardware** — the Tengucon 2025 listing explicitly names EV chargers, automotive ECUs and IVI systems; the Hardwear.io NL 2026 listing adds demonstrations of real exploits on vehicle systems and charging infrastructure.
- **Community output** — talks and badge projects; the site keeps a talk archive covering DEF CON 27 (2019) through DEF CON 34 (2026).

Recent and upcoming appearances on the calendar (as checked 2026-11-17) include CODE BLUE 2026 (17–18 November, Tokyo), Hardwear.io NL 2026 (19–20 November, Amsterdam — the first time CHV joins that event), DEF CON 34 (6–9 August 2026, Las Vegas), DEF CON Singapore 2026 (28–30 April), Tengucon 2025, CODE BLUE 2025, Defcamp 2025 and Ekoparty 2025.

## Limits and safety notes

The site is an **events calendar and sponsor/talk-call vehicle**, not a repository of self-study material: the value is largely in attending, and several listings are promotional or forward-looking. Talk captions and slides, where published by the host conferences, are the durable artefacts — this KB links to a few of them above. The status is **active** rather than published because the programme is ongoing. No event was attended and no hardware used for this page.
