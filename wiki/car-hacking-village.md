Non-profit running hands-on car hacking workshops and CTFs at DEF CON, Black Hat, HITCON, CODE BLUE, and other conferences.

The Car Hacking Village is the community non-profit that runs the hands-on car hacking areas at security conferences: beginner workshops, CTF competitions, live automotive reverse engineering, and open hacking benches with real ECUs, infotainment systems, and EV chargers. Its flagship is DEF CON in Las Vegas, with regular appearances at CODE BLUE (Tokyo), HITCON, Ekoparty, Defcamp, Hardwear.io, and BSides events.

This is the entry for people who learn by doing: almost everything the KB documents in text can be practised in person at a village, with volunteers and sponsors' hardware on hand. The caveat is the flip side — the value is in attending. The website is an events calendar and sponsor/talk calls, not a repository of material to work through at home.
