CAN bus ID reference and documentation.

A community-collected database of CAN IDs and payload meanings, organised by brand and model — Acura through Volvo, plus common/OBD material — maintained by iDoka. This is the page to check before reverse engineering a car's traffic from scratch: if someone has already decoded your model's frames, they are probably noted here, and the list is CC0, so what you find is free to reuse.

Caveats: it is crowd-sourced and it shows — coverage is deep for popular models and thin elsewhere, entries arrive from many contributors with no central verification, and a listed payload meaning is a lead to confirm on your own bench, not ground truth.
