David Ward and Paul Wooderson, SAE International 2021. Short introduction to ISO/SAE 21434 covering cybersecurity basics, setting up a company process, and assurance and certification.

A short, deliberately introductory primer on ISO/SAE 21434, the road-vehicle cybersecurity engineering standard. The authors are HORIBA MIRA specialists — Ward leads its functional safety team, Wooderson its cybersecurity team, and Wooderson was a nominated UK expert to the ISO/SAE joint working group that developed the standard. Coverage: what cybersecurity means for automotive cyber-physical systems, establishing a cybersecurity process for your company, and assurance and certification, plus a chapter of frequently asked questions.

The right first read for engineers and managers who need to orient before tackling the standard itself or standing up a CSMS. Caveat: it is an introduction, not a clause-by-clause commentary — for worked method depth, pair it with the TARA book below; for engineering practice, with Nasser's handbook.
