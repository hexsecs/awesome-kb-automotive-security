Video training on Automotive MACsec concepts by Technica Engineering.

Technica Engineering's free video training on MACsec in vehicle networks. One wrinkle worth knowing before you click: the linked video is actually titled "What is Automotive MKA?" — in it, Technica's Technical Fellow Dr. Lars Völker explains the MACsec Key Agreement protocol that protects messages on automotive Ethernet, as the direct sequel to the channel's earlier "What is MACsec?" video. Expect the problems automotive MKA had to solve, comparisons with IT-industry practice, and worked examples.

It is for engineers meeting MACsec for the first time in an automotive Ethernet context who want the key-agreement half explained plainly. Caveat: it is vendor-produced training — Technica sells automotive Ethernet tooling and services — so the teaching is solid but the framing is a vendor's.
