Open vehicle research collective behind the Car Hacker's Handbook, publishing the free edition of the book, ICSim, and workshop material for hands-on vehicle security practice.

OpenGarages is the open vehicle-research collective whose best-known output is The Car Hacker's Handbook: the book's free Creative Commons edition is published on this site (read online or as PDF; the print edition is sold by No Starch Press), alongside the collective's other hands-on material, including the ICSim instrument-cluster simulator used across the field for safe CAN practice without a car.

For newcomers this remains the classic free on-ramp — the handbook plus a simulator covers most of the CAN fundamentals the rest of the KB assumes. Caveat: the website itself is now essentially a landing page for the book; the living artefacts are the book text and the GitHub projects (ICSim is at zombieCraig/ICSim), not the site.
