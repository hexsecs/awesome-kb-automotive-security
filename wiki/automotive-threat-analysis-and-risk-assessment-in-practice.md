Rodrigo do Carmo and Alexander Schlensog, Springer 2024. Practitioner's guide to threat analysis and risk assessment (TARA) following ISO/SAE 21434, with worked examples for automotive embedded and IT/OT systems.

A method book on actually performing a TARA under ISO/SAE 21434, written by two secunet consultants who lead TARA projects and sit on ISO/DIN committees. It covers the European cybersecurity legal and regulatory framework, scoping a TARA, threat-modeling approaches and catalogs, attack-feasibility rating, and risk evaluation, with detailed case studies across three domains: in-vehicle E/E systems, the vehicle's connected ecosystem, and manufacturing plants — drawing on ISO/IEC 27000 and IEC 62443 where the work crosses into IT/OT.

For practitioners who have to produce TARAs that survive audit, this is the worked-examples companion to Ward and Wooderson's introductory primer. Caveats: the framing is European regulation and the ISO/SAE 21434 method specifically, and the focus is process rigour rather than vehicle technology. 259 pages, 9 chapters.
