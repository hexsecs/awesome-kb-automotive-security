Curated specifications, tools, and resources for EV charging protocols including OCPP, ISO 15118, OCPI, and Eichrecht.

Julien Herr's list of EV charging specifications, tools, and resources: the protocol documents and implementations for OCPP, ISO 15118, OCPI, OICP, eMIP, and Germany's Eichrecht calibration law, split into a specifications half and a tools half. It is written for developers and e-mobility engineers rather than security researchers — which is exactly why it belongs here: it is the map of the standards the KB's charging-attack pages are about.

If a charging paper or tool in this KB names a protocol behaviour you don't recognise, this list is where the specification and the reference implementations live. Caveat: it catalogues the ecosystem as its vendors publish it; for the security reading of the same protocols, come back to this KB's EV Charging pages.
