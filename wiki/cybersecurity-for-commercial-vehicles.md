Gloria D'Anna, SAE International 2018. Covers heavy vehicle and fleet security, how it differs from passenger cars, SAE J3061, platooning, and breach forensics.

The book-length treatment of the commercial-vehicle segment: a multi-authored SAE volume led by Gloria D'Anna, with subject-matter experts from industry, academia, and government. Its key subject areas are SAE J3061 (the cybersecurity guidebook for cyber-physical vehicle systems), how commercial-vehicle cybersecurity differs from passenger cars, forensics for identifying breaches, platooning and fleet implications, and the social, policy, and privacy dimensions of securing fleets and cargo.

For engineers and managers in trucking, fleet operations, and heavy-vehicle supply chains — the segment most other books on this list treat as an afterthought. Caveat: published in 2018, it is anchored on J3061, which ISO/SAE 21434 has since superseded as the reference framework; read its process material through that lens. 316 pages.
