Ahmad MK Nasser and Dennis Kengo Oka, Packt 2026. The two handbook authors together on the offensive side, covering exploitation of modern automotive platforms.

The offensive counterpart to Nasser's 2023 engineering handbook (subtitle: *An engineering handbook for exploiting modern automotive platforms*), pairing him with Dennis Kengo Oka of *Building Secure Cars*. It examines the whole connected-vehicle ecosystem through an offensive lens — cloud backends and wireless protocols down through in-vehicle networks, high-performance computers, ECUs, and physical sensors — and works the full penetration-testing lifecycle: active and passive reconnaissance, firmware reverse engineering, and building complex attack chains from real-world exploits, closing with resilient design and an AI-enabled secure engineering lifecycle.

For cybersecurity professionals, automotive engineers, security testers, and researchers who already have the defensive/process picture and want the exploitation side. Caveat: a May 2026 Packt title — current, but new and practitioner-formatted, so treat it as a field guide rather than a reference standard. 564 pages, the longest book on this list.
