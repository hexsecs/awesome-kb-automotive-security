Alfred Basta and Sam Hana, Kendall Hunt 2026. Textbook covering threats from ECU networks and sensor spoofing to OTA patching, EV charging breaches, and incident response.

A higher-education textbook that surveys the whole field in 25 chapters: vehicle architecture and attack surface, threat actors and case studies (the 2015 Jeep hack, EV charging breaches, sensor spoofing), the regulatory landscape (UN R155/R156, ISO/SAE 21434 — including a deep-dive chapter with TARA), CAN and Ethernet security, ECU and firmware security, telematics and OTA updates, infotainment, AI/ML and sensor security, supply chain, incident response and coordinated disclosure, privacy, Level 3+ autonomy, and EV charging infrastructure.

Written for students, instructors, and professionals who want one current volume across the entire syllabus. Caveats: the breadth is the point and the limit — each topic gets a chapter, not a deep treatment — and as a 2026 Kendall Hunt higher-ed title it is sold mainly through academic channels, so it has no track record yet.
