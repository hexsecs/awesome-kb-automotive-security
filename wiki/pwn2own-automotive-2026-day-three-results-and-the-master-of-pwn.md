Zero Day Initiative, Tokyo 2026. Final results of the contest, where $1,047,000 was awarded for 76 unique zero-days in infotainment systems and EV chargers.

This entry is the Zero Day Initiative's official final-results post for Pwn2Own Automotive 2026, held at Automotive World in Tokyo in January 2026 — filed in this section as the contest's primary public record. Across the three days, contestants were awarded $1,047,000 for 76 unique zero-day vulnerabilities against production infotainment systems (Alpine, Kenwood, and Sony head units) and EV chargers (Autel, ChargePoint, Grizzl-E, Alpitronic, Phoenix Contact, and others). The Master of Pwn title went to Fuzzware.io — Tobias Scharnowski, Felix Buchmann, and Kristian Covic — with 28 points and $215,500 in winnings. The day's most quoted result was Juurin Oy's exploit of an Alpitronic HYC50 fast charger via a TOCTOU bug, celebrated by installing a playable Doom on it.

What you get: a results blog post — an entry-by-entry scoreboard of who exploited what, with payouts and points — not technical write-ups. Under Pwn2Own rules the vulnerabilities are reported to the affected vendors through ZDI, and exploit detail appears only later, if at all, in ZDI advisories and follow-on talks. Use this page as the map of which product classes fell at the 2026 contest; pair it with ZDI's day-one and day-two results posts for the complete picture, and with the individual teams' later publications for technique.
