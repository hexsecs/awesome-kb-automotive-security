Structured learning path from CAN and UDS fundamentals through TARA, firmware reverse engineering, and hardware hacking into specialized career tracks.

A single-document career roadmap on GitHub (AutoSecurityy org), staged from beginner to specialist: CS and networking basics first, then automotive protocols (CAN, ISO-TP, UDS, LIN, FlexRay, Automotive Ethernet), TARA and ISO/SAE 21434 / UN R155, then penetration testing, firmware reverse engineering, and hardware hacking, with named tools to learn at each step (CANoe, SavvyCAN, ICSim, PlayUDS, and similar). It is aimed at students and career switchers targeting analyst, penetration-tester, consultant, or engineer roles.

Honest caveat: it was published in one burst over a few days around New Year 2025 and has not been updated since, so treat it as a static map of what to learn rather than a maintained course — the topic sequence is sound, but tool recommendations will age and there is no community or instruction behind it.
