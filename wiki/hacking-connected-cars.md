Alissa Knight, Wiley 2020. Tactics, techniques, and procedures for penetration testing, threat modelling, and risk assessment of telematics control units and infotainment systems.

A field manual (subtitle: *Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures*) codifying the author's decade of penetration-testing connected cars for OEMs. Part I walks the engagement lifecycle — pre-engagement and lab setup, intelligence gathering, threat modeling (STRIDE, VAST, PASTA), vulnerability analysis, exploitation (including rogue cellular base stations and hunting the TCU), and post-exploitation — aimed mainly at telematics control units and infotainment/head units. Part II covers risk management: risk-assessment frameworks (HEAVENS, EVITA), PKI in automotive/VANETs, and reporting, with downloadable scope and rules-of-engagement templates from the companion site.

For penetration testers, red teamers, and risk assessors. Caveats the author states herself: this is not a deep treatment of in-vehicle networking or CAN fundamentals (see the Car Hacker's Handbook and Voss for those), and the wireless/cellular techniques reflect the TCU landscape of 2020. 272 pages.
