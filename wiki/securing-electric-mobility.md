Aparna Kumari (ed.), Elsevier 2026. Edited volume on the security of electric vehicles and charging infrastructure.

An edited academic volume on the EV side of the field, edited by Aparna Kumari (Nirma University, India). Its 13 chapters cover EV anatomy and connectivity, the threat landscape and countermeasures, blockchain-based secure data communication, EV charging challenges and cybersecurity issues, lightweight cryptographic primitives for intelligent EV systems, and real-world e-mobility case studies, alongside the fundamentals readers need on how EVs and their infrastructure fit together.

For graduate students, researchers, and practitioners whose focus is electric vehicles and charging rather than the vehicle platform as a whole. Caveat: as an edited volume it is uneven — a few chapters (energy-efficient path planning, smart-parking planning, inductive-charging design) are mobility/energy topics only tangentially about security.
