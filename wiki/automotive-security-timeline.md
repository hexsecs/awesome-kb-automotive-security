Continuously updated knowledge base of automotive cybersecurity events including vulnerability disclosures, attack demonstrations, Pwn2Own Automotive results, and supply-chain incidents.

An open-source chronological archive of automotive security events — vulnerability disclosures, research publications, attack demonstrations, conference results (Black Hat, DEF CON, Pwn2Own Automotive), data breaches, and OEM/supplier supply-chain incidents — each entry dated and referenced. It exists because this history is otherwise scattered across advisories, blogs, and conference programmes; here it is searchable in one timeline, in English and Chinese.

It is community-maintained (additions arrive as issues and pull requests) and aimed at researchers, threat-intelligence work, and anyone preparing a talk or paper who needs the field's history with citations. One licensing caveat: the maintainers allow non-commercial use with attribution but reserve commercial use (products, paid training, paid intel reports) for separate licensing.
